# Community conduct

Ember is a small, public technical project. Participants in its issues, pull requests and other maintainer-managed spaces are expected to:

- Treat people respectfully, including newcomers and people who disagree about development tools.
- Critique code, measurements and claims with concrete examples; avoid personal attacks.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, spam and publishing another person's private information.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project. Honest disclosure of AI use is part of this project's documentation; contributions should be evaluated with reproducible evidence.
- Respect third-party licenses and give appropriate credit.

The maintainer, [bbauti](https://github.com/bbauti), may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation or block accounts when necessary to uphold these expectations. Enforcement should be proportionate to the conduct and context.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for abuse. For a matter requiring a private maintainer conversation, request a private contact without posting sensitive details publicly. Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
